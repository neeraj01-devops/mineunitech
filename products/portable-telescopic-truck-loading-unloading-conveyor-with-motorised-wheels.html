Product Overview

Designed for warehouses where flexibility is paramount, the Self-Propelled Telescopic Conveyor eliminates the need for dedicated conveyors at every bay. A single operator can effortlessly drive the unit between dock doors using an intuitive motorized tiller control.

Once positioned at the truck door, the boom extends into the container while height-adjusting hydraulics align the conveyor with the operator's ideal working posture, maximizing ergonomics and packing density.

Designed for efficiency. Built for a sustainable tomorrow.

Engineering Features

  • Integrated high-torque electric transaxle drive wheels allow effortless bay-to-bay mobility.
  • Dead-man safety braking and anti-pinching tiller head safeguard operating personnel.
  • Bi-directional belt drive enables seamless transition between loading and unloading cycles.
  • Robust hydraulic scissor lifting arms support full loading capacities at maximum elevation.
  • Quick-connect power umbilical with automatic cord reel or autonomous battery traction.
