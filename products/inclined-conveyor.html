Key Specifications

Conveyor Type Long-Span Truss Inclined Conveyor
Frame Open web structural steel truss for maximum rigidity
Belt High-traction rough-top vulcanized rubber / PVC belt
Incline Angle 10° – 35°
Span Length 6 m – 25 m unsupported spans
Belt Width 400 mm – 1000 mm
Drive High efficiency shaft-mounted helical gearmotor with backstop
Load Capacity Up to 300 kg/m
Application Warehouse mezzanine transfer, loading dock to sorting floor

Product Overview

The Industrial Inclined Conveyor is constructed using open-web structural steel truss sections, providing tremendous bending strength over long unsupported spans. It is designed to bridge significant vertical heights between factory floors, elevated packing mezzanines, and high-level sortation systems.

Featuring a high-friction vulcanized rough-top rubber belt and heavy-duty shaft-mounted gearmotors with integrated backstops, it guarantees dependable non-slip conveyance of continuous product streams.

Designed for efficiency. Built for a sustainable tomorrow.
Learn More

Engineering Features

  • Deep structural truss framework allows long spans between building support columns.
  • Integrated mechanical backstop brake prevents reverse belt roll during power outages.
  • Heavy-duty return rollers with protective guarding ensure safe, smooth belt recirculation.
  • High-traction rubber belt handles heavy corrugated cartons, totes, and packaged goods.
  • Optional full-length maintenance walkways and safety netting for overhead elevated spans.
